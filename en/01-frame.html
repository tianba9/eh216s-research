Ask the Right Question First

This chapter does not merely search; it turns “research” into something testable. Four propositions are each tied to a criterion that data could overturn, and twenty sources are graded by what they can and cannot support.

01From task to falsifiable claims

The assignment asks for research on a flying-vehicle technology. That phrase is too vague. It is split into four propositions, each with a concrete disconfirming condition.

Table 1-1　Propositions and their tests
No.PropositionWould be overturned if…Verdict
P1The published figures are physically self-consistentDerived endurance falls well below the claim, or the thrust-to-weight ratio failsHolds
P2The configuration confines the market to short point-to-point tripsA commercial commuter route exists with adequate daily capacityHolds
P3Order volume reflects industrial policy, not end-user demandBuyers are end consumers and most orders carry depositsHolds
P4Battery specific energy is the only variable that changes P2Another constraint is relieved at a comparable scalePartly holds

Method matters: credibility depends on whether a reader can follow the same path, not on the length of the argument.

02Search protocol

Searches were run in Chinese and English, prioritising official disclosures, standards, academia and finally media. Media adds chronology and competitor status, not authoritative parameters.

03Screening and inclusion

Twenty sources form the evidence matrix. Four exclusion rules were applied: unattributable reposts, self-contradictory data without explanation, purely promotional copy, and material about a different EH216 variant.

04Evidence grading and conflict handling

Five labels appear inline throughout the volume: official, standard, academic, media and author's estimate. Three rules govern conflicts: within one dimension use the official figure; never merge different statistical calibers; keep forecasts labelled as forecasts.

05Method limitations

No interviews were conducted. Empty weight, rotor diameter and pack capacity are undisclosed, so the power calculation is a bounded estimate rather than a design result. Commercial judgement is time-sensitive: ticketed operation began only in March 2026.

06Chapter Summary

This chapter does not simply collect sources; it makes research testable. Four propositions each carry a criterion that data could overturn; searches are graded by source authority; twenty sources are labelled by what they can support.

Credibility depends on reproducibility, not on the length of the argument.
