01Five Key Conclusions

The five claims below carry the main argument of this research. Each is labelled with the grade of evidence supporting it and with what it cannot be used to support.

Conclusion 01 · Competitive moat

The real moat is not the configuration but the certification evidence chain

The EH216-S is the only passenger-carrying eVTOL in the world holding TC, AC, PC and OC simultaneously Official. As of September 2026, Joby is in the final stage of the FAA's five-stage certification, with 20% of applicant work completed and 10% accepted by the authority; Archer closed stage three in April 2026, and neither has obtained a type certificate Media analysis. The multirotor configuration can be copied; the failure-probability argument for three flight-control sets and the 90,000-sortie operational data cannot.

Certification evidence

Conclusion 02 · Scenario boundary

The parameters lock its market into “experience”, not “commuting”

A 30 km range, 25 minutes of endurance and roughly 120 minutes of recharge Manufacturer disclosure. On a calculation of 25 minutes of flight plus 120 minutes of recharge plus 10 minutes of turnaround, the theoretical daily sorties per aircraft are about three, with fewer than 2,000 seat-offerings per year Author's estimate. This turnover rate cannot support the capacity density a commuting product requires; it can only support point-to-point sightseeing at scenic sites.

Operating conditions

Conclusion 03 · Demand quality

Order volume holds up; the reality of demand is not yet verified

More than 1,000 indicative orders are on hand, and over 1,100 batch orders domestically Company via media. But the customers are mainly local state-owned platforms and financial leasing companies, and most indicative orders carry no deposit Media investigation. Such orders reflect local industrial-policy intent, not end-user travel demand.

Order evidence

Conclusion 04 · Financial state

It has crossed “can we sell” but not “can we earn”

In 2025 revenue reached RMB 509.5m, up 11.7% year on year, with a 62.0% gross margin and 221 aircraft delivered; the full-year net loss was RMB 231m. Q4 net profit was RMB 10.5m, the first GAAP quarterly profit since listing Financial report. Scale effects show signs of an inflection point but do not yet constitute steady-state profitability.

Aircraft-level economics calculator

Conclusion 05 · Decisive variable

Battery specific energy affects endurance; economics also depend on turnaround and demand

The company reported a 48 min 10 s EH216-S test flight with a solid-state battery in November 2024. The 480 Wh/kg figure applies to cells. This flight provides evidence of longer endurance in the tested configuration. Usable energy and life of production packs and commercial operating performance still need separate evidence. Operating economics also depend on recharge time, fare and occupancy.

02Research Overview

This report is in two volumes. Volume I asks “can it be built, and who buys it”; its sister volume, Full-Spectrum Safety Analysis of the EH216-S (Volume II), asks “why is it safe, and why did the regulator accept it”. Both volumes share the same factual baseline and can be read independently or side by side.

Why study it

The EH216-S is not the best-performing passenger-carrying eVTOL; it is the only one that has completed the full certification process and entered ticketed operation. Studying it bypasses the hype around “concept-vehicle figures” and tests directly what an engineering path already confirmed by the regulator does and does not support. For judging the deployment pace of the whole category, this is more valuable than comparing whose range is longer.

Three rules for handling data

  1. Conflicts within one dimension: use the official value. The manufacturer's site gives a maximum take-off weight of 620 kg while secondary sources mostly give 650 kg; this report uses 620 kg and retains a note on the conflict.
  2. Different calibers are never merged. Three readings of low-altitude-economy scale exist — RMB 715.1bn, RMB 859.2bn and RMB 1.5tn — with different statistical boundaries; they are presented separately and never added.
  3. Forecasts stay labelled as forecasts. The “RMB 2tn by 2030” figure is an institutional projection; when cited, its forecast status and issuing body are retained.

Three self-imposed constraints

  • No chase after single-point forecasts. Market size is given as a range with its caliber, not as one attractive number.
  • No smoothing over conflicts. Official and secondary figures are presented side by side, with the reasons for the choice explained.
  • Distinguish “verified” from “claimed”. Holding all four certificates is a verifiable regulatory fact; 90,000 sorties with zero accidents is a company claim.
