EH216-S: The First Certificate That Turned “Can It Fly” into “Can It Ship”

This is not a product brochure. It asks a question that can be falsified: how did a two-seat, pilotless aircraft with a 30 km range and 25 minutes of endurance become the first passenger-carrying eVTOL to obtain a Chinese type certificate in 2023, only to begin selling sightseeing tickets in 2026? The report traces its technical, operational and commercial boundaries with a single chain of evidence, stating explicitly which conclusions have support and which are inference.

01Five Key Conclusions

These five claims carry the argument. Each states its supporting evidence and the limits of what it can prove.

01 · Competitive moat

The barrier is the certification record, not the configuration

The EH216-S is the only passenger eVTOL holding all four certificates. Joby and Archer were still inside their certification processes in 2026. The multi-rotor layout can be copied; the failure-probability argument behind three flight-control channels and 90,000 sorties cannot.

02 · Scenario boundary

The parameters lock it into experiences, not commuting

A 30 km range, 25 minutes of endurance and roughly 120 minutes of recharge give about three sorties per aircraft per day and fewer than 2,000 seat-offerings per year. That is an order of magnitude below what urban commuting requires.

03 · Demand quality

Order volume is real; end-user demand is not yet proven

More than 1,000 indicative orders are reported, but buyers are largely local state-owned platforms and leasing companies, and most indicative orders carry no deposit. These orders reflect industrial-policy intent rather than travel demand.

04 · Financial state

It crossed “can we sell” but not “can we earn”

In 2025 revenue reached RMB 509.5m (+11.7%) with a 62.0% gross margin, yet a net loss of RMB 231m. Q4 produced the first GAAP quarterly profit. Scale effects are appearing, but they are not yet a steady state.

05 · Decisive variable

Battery specific energy is the only variable that changes the answer

A November 2024 test with 480 Wh/kg cells extended endurance to 48 minutes. If that reaches production, the scenario boundary moves from sightseeing to intercity shuttle; if not, the 30 km line stays put.

02Research Overview

This report has two volumes. Volume I asks “can it be built, and who buys it”; Volume II, Full-Spectrum Safety Analysis, asks “why is it safe, and why did the regulator accept it”. Both share the same factual baseline and can be read independently.

Why study it

The EH216-S is not the best-performing eVTOL. It is the only one that has completed the full certification process and entered ticketed service. Studying it removes the noise of concept vehicles and tests what an already-approved engineering path actually supports.

Three rules for handling data

  1. Conflicts within one dimension: use the official value. 620 kg from the manufacturer is used over 650 kg from secondary tables, with both retained in the record.
  2. Different calibers are never merged. Three market-size readings are shown separately, never added.
  3. Forecasts stay labelled as forecasts. A 2030 projection remains a projection, with its issuer named.
