Certification or Range: Pick One

Certification progress and performance form a trade-off curve; no product occupies both ends.

01Competitive Landscape

Joby and Archer lead in performance and remain inside FAA certification. AeroHT's modular design compresses certification to the flying module. EH216-S is the only one already in revenue service.

02Five-Dimension Comparison

EH216-S does not lead in total score: 15 against 18 for its US peers. Yet within the subset “currently operable” it is the only option.

03Does the Moat Hold

Partly. Certification evidence cannot be transferred, but the lead is only three to four years. A tilt-rotor with twice the range could erase its niche.

04Risk Register

Five risks: slow order conversion, unprofitable operations, a regulator-confirmed incident, competitor certification by 2028, and local-budget tightening.

05Overall Judgment

The moat buys entry, not permanence. The right use of the window is to lock in operators and customers rather than chase parameters.

06Chapter Summary

The rating is B, conditional on three things: stay in sightseeing and short shuttle, cut recharge below 60 minutes, and build the operating network before 2028.
