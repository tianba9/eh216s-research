From a Certificate to a Business

Seven chapters and twenty sources converge on one judgment: the value of EH216-S lies not in its range but in proving this path can be certified.

01One Thread: Three Questions

Can it be built? Yes, and the boundary is structural. Who buys it? Scenic sites and short shuttles, not commuters. Why does it win? Certification lead, not performance.

02Three Key Findings

The parameters are credible. The commercial model is not closed. The moat is a certificate, not a technology.

03Limits of the Conclusions

Third-party recomputation, missing key parameters, and a market model without real passenger data.

04Final Position

It is not a great aircraft; it is a scarce certificate. Its present is set by certification, its near future by recharge time, and its distant future by batteries.
