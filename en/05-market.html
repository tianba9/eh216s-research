02Capacity: The Daily Sortie Ceiling

This is the most important calculation in the chapter and the most easily overlooked. Endurance is only 25 minutes but recharge takes 120 minutes, so flying accounts for just 16% of one operating cycle.

What does 1800 seats per year mean? An 8-metre tour boat carries that many in a single day. Capacity density is an order of magnitude below what commuting requires — which is why "capacity matching" scores only 1 in Chapter 04.

The most important finding of this model

In the cost structure, depreciation, personnel, site and insurance together account for 70%, all annual fixed costs independent of how many sorties are flown. This means raising utilisation is the only path to lower cost — and the only lever for that is shortening recharge time. The next section quantifies this through sensitivity analysis.

05Market Size: Three Calibers, Not Interchangeable

Published figures for the size of the low-altitude economy differ by hundreds of billions of yuan. This is not a data error but a difference in statistical boundaries. Citing a figure without stating its caliber creates confusion.

Caliber20252026Long termStatistical boundary

Note: the three cannot be added together or substituted for one another. The narrow caliber suits describing the "core low-altitude economy industry"; the broad caliber suits the overall picture of the "low-altitude economy and related industries". Research institute

A counter-signal that must be written into the conclusion

From September 2026, Hangzhou withdrew its draft "21 measures for low altitude", while Guangzhou's Tianhe and Shenzhen's Guangming districts suspended direct subsidies for the low-altitude economy. The policy logic is not a lack of confidence in the industry but a shift of funds from "subsidising firms" to "building infrastructure, setting up industry funds and government procurement of services". This means firms dependent on subsidies and lacking real scenarios will be weeded out faster. Media investigation

Put together with the duty-cycle conclusion of Chapter 03, the real constraint on the market becomes clear: the industry's scale narrative is policy-driven, while unit economics is determined by turnover rate; the two are not synchronised.

06Demand: Order Structure Matters More Than Count

The order figures in public reports are striking: over 1100 bulk orders domestically and over 1000 indicative orders on hand. But the composition of those orders determines their real value.

The 2025 delivery curve confirms this structural instability: 221 aircraft for the year, of which 100 were delivered in Q4 alone and only 121 in the first three quarters. The company attributes this to delayed customer payments causing an order backlog, with revenue recognised concentrically in Q4. Financial report This "low first, high later" recognition pattern shows demand is still project-based and batch-driven, without a steady shipment rhythm.

A judgment

Current orders reflect local industrial-layout intent: buyers are mostly local state-owned platforms and tourism enterprises purchasing a "low-altitude economy calling card" rather than capacity. Such demand is real, but its scale is set by fiscal budgets rather than travel demand — which explains why orders are highly concentrated in regions with policy levers.

07Chapter Verdict

Verdict 1

The market-size narrative holds, but is unrelated to unit economics

A trillion-yuan market is the result of industrial policy; unit economics is the result of turnover rate. The former cannot prove the latter; the two must be discussed separately.

Verdict 2

The current model is essentially selling equipment, not capacity

The baseline break-even fare is about RMB 556 at full occupancy. Reported fares of RMB 299–498 fall below that threshold even with both seats sold.

Verdict 3

Recharge time is a more urgent bottleneck than battery energy density

Energy density determines how far it can fly; recharge time determines how many times it can fly per year. With fixed costs at 70%, the latter has a greater effect on profitability and is easier to address through engineering.

08Chapter Summary

This chapter replaces macro narrative with the accounts of one aircraft. The three market-size calibers are listed separately, not added together; the order structure shows buyers are local state-owned platforms and lessors, not end passengers.
