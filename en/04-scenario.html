Flying Is Not Operating

Scenarios are combinations of hazard and threshold, not marketing categories. The same aircraft is a product above a scenic park and a risk above a city.

01Scenario Matrix

Five scenario classes were placed by regulatory access and demand rigidity. Sightseeing and short shuttle sit in the upper-right quadrant; commuting is demand-strong but regulatory-weak.

02Five-Dimension Scoring

Sightseeing scores 17 out of 20, shuttle 16, emergency 14, advertising 13 and commuting 11. The spread is structural, not marginal.

03Seven ODD Elements

Airspace, weather, vertiport, daylight, population density, communications and ground access define the operating design domain. Each is a boundary on the safety case.

04The Cost of Segregated Airspace

No networking, no mixed operations and no city-centre access. Scheduled service is impossible; onlypre-booked, pre-booked experience flights fit.

05Deployment Path

Demonstration first, then cross-area shuttle, then urban commuting. Each step requires a different external condition, and the third needs an energy or configuration breakthrough.

06Chapter Summary

Sightseeing and short shuttle are the only closed-loop combination today. Segregated airspace caps the model, and door-to-door time erases the speed advantage over driving.
