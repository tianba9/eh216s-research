A general reminder for all estimates

The mass budget in Chapter 02, the power calculation in Chapter 03 and the unit economics model in Chapter 05 all rest on the assumptions listed above. These estimates serve to judge order of magnitude and identify constraints, not to give precise answers. If measured data become available, replace the assumptions and recalculate — the report has written out all formulae and assumptions precisely to make this actionable.

04Reproduction Notes

All calculations in this report can be recomputed by hand; the core formulae are as follows:

  • Hover induced power: Pi = G1.5 / √(2ρA), where G is gross weight, ρ air density and A total disc area.
  • Disc loading: G / A, taken as 34.3 kg/m² in this report.
  • Energy per sortie: the sum of power integrated over time for each flight phase, giving 40.8 kWh in this report.
  • Daily sortie ceiling: floor(operating window / cycle time), here floor(480 / 155) = 3.
  • Cost per seat: total annual cost / (annual sorties × 2).
