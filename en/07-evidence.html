Every Claim Traces Back to a Source

This chapter is the base of the report: twenty sources, five credibility levels, and an explicit statement of what each source can support.

01Evidence Matrix

Each entry lists type, date, credibility and the claim it supports. Official and regulatory sources carry the most weight; company claims are marked as such.

02References

Format follows GB/T 7714. Regulator documents, company filings, industry research and media reports are listed with access dates.

03Calculation Assumptions

Empty weight, rotor diameter and pack capacity are undisclosed. Every estimate states its assumptions so the reader can substitute their own numbers.

04Reproduction Notes

Hover induced power uses G^1.5 / sqrt(2ρA); daily sorties use floor(480 / cycle time); cost per seat divides annual cost by annual seats.

05Chapter Summary

The point is not “I am right” but “you can recalculate”. Formulae, assumptions and conflict rules are all written down.
